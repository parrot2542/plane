Summit Departure

You are parked on a plateau at the top of a mountain. Run down the flat top, lift off over the edge, and fly the endless country below: forests, lakes, ridges and snow. Other aircraft share the sky, and hitting one ends your flight. Climb high enough and the sky turns black. A Moon floats 100,000 m up, north of the summit, and you can land on it. Runways with control towers sit every 20 to 30 km in every direction, and the HUD shows the nearest one. Day and night each last five minutes. Towns glow at night, and townspeople wave when you pass low and slow, under 100 m and 150 km/h.
